### F-02 Mods can pre-empt the wrapper's PreToolUse and PermissionRequest relay

- Date: 2026-11-09. Verified: primary (release notes, mods admin doc).
- Sources: [v2.1.321](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.321),
  [mods admin](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/admin.md)
- What: non-managed PreToolUse hooks run after the last mod; a mod that answers a call skips them. Claude-written mods
  now turn on without asking in auto/bypass, and the sec-default guard does not load with our OAuth-token login.
- Applies: claude-code-wrapper bootstrap/settings.py and Dockerfile: managed-settings.json with the relay hooks and
  `allowManagedModsOnly: true`. New proposal P1.
- Effort M; risk med (managed settings change overlay precedence).

### F-03 idleCompaction compacts idle leaders while background work runs

- Date: 2026-10-31. Verified: primary (issue, settings reference).
- Sources: [issue 106402](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/106402),
  [settings reference](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference.md)
- What: sessions with 200K+ context are compacted after about 54-60 minutes idle, even while a background agent
  works, so a waiting leader loses detail. Off switch `idleCompaction: false`.
- Applies: claude-code-wrapper bootstrap/settings.py and orchestrator presets, per role; reconcile with the wrapper's
  own idle auto-compact in router.py. Updates T-90.
- Effort S; risk low.

### F-04 Agent view / background daemon can move a session out of the wrapper PTY

### F-06 Stop-hook loops have no built-in guard

- Date: 2026-11-04. Verified: primary (issues).
- Sources: [107256](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/107256),
  [107915](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/107915),
  [109284](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/109284)
- What: a blocking Stop hook plus a denying PreToolUse hook looped six days headless (about 10,000 refused edits);
  `/goal` re-prompts every few seconds for hours.
- Applies: wrapper Stop relay (cap continuations on `stop_hook_active`) and an orchestrator per-session turn/token
  ceiling for trigger runs. New proposal P2; informs T-70, T-88, T-89.
- Effort M; risk low.

### F-10 Handoff summaries invent approvals
